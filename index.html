About National Security Wing

Built on discipline. Run on accountability.

Founded in Hubballi and led by professionals with deep operational experience, National Security Wing was established to raise the standard of private security in North Karnataka. We are not a manpower agency that happens to provide guards — we are a security organisation that engineers protection.

Every officer who wears the NSW emblem is recruited against background-verified criteria, trained in our in-house programme, and supervised through a rigorous chain of command. Our clients include central government departments, premier hospitals, large educational societies, and respected real estate developers — institutions that cannot afford a lapse.

  • Background verification on every candidate before deployment
  • Site-specific training tailored to your security plan
  • 24×7 control room with supervisor escalation matrix
  • Statutory compliance — PF, ESIC, bonus, and gratuity

Why NSW

Seven reasons institutions in Hubli choose us.

01 / DISCIPLINE

Military-style training

Drill, posture, salutation, alertness drills, and emergency response — every recruit goes through a structured ten-day induction before deployment.

02 / VETTING

100% police verification

No officer is placed on a client site without complete antecedent checks, address verification, and Aadhaar-validated documentation.

03 / COMPLIANCE

PSARA & ISO certified

Full statutory cover — PSARA license, ISO 9001:2015 quality system, ESIC and PF compliance, and labour department registration.

04 / SUPERVISION

Layered command structure

Guard → supervisor → field officer → operations manager. Every site sees a supervisor visit, not an absent contractor.

05 / TECHNOLOGY

Digital attendance & patrol

Biometric attendance, GPS-based patrol checkpoints, and real-time incident reporting — clients receive transparent monthly MIS.

06 / RESPONSE

24×7 emergency desk

Our control room is staffed round the clock. Any incident on any site is escalated, documented, and resolved within defined SLAs.

07 / RETENTION

Welfare-first workforce

Timely salaries, statutory benefits, and welfare programmes mean lower attrition — and a guard who knows your premises matters.

08 / LOCAL ROOTS

Hubli-headquartered

We live where we work. Local presence means faster mobilisation, accountable leadership, and genuine community knowledge.
